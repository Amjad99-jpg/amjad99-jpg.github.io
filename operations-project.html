03

How the process ran

Step 1

Plan by season

Planned every order around student term times and summer.

Why: Demand peaked in those weeks, so orders had to follow it.
Step 2

Order to budget

Placed each order against the budget, best sellers first.

Why: To keep cash out of slow stock and never run out of best sellers.
Step 3

Check every line

Checked each delivery for the right item, quantity and price.

Why: Short or missing items were the most common mismatch.
Step 4

Match the records

Matched the invoice against point of sale, inventory and CRM records.

Why: So we only paid for what arrived and every record stayed accurate.
Step 5

Close the gap

Traced the cause, resolved it with the supplier and updated the KPI tracker.

Why: To stop the same mismatch happening again.

What I’d change next

How it ran

  • Every line checked one at a time
  • Each mismatch chased separately
  • Accurate, but manual

Proposed redesign

  • A matching sheet that flags any gap automatically
  • A supplier scorecard for supplier reviews
  • Reorder levels that rise ahead of term time and summer

Proposals for the future, not changes made at the time.
