About me

From live TV to business analysis

I began presenting children's and youth programmes on television and radio in Oman at a young age and progressed to national affairs, interviewing senior public figures, education leaders and government ministers live. It taught me what business analysis relies on: asking precise questions, listening to stakeholders and checking every fact before it goes out.

In the UK I completed an MSc in International Business and Management and built my business analysis experience. As an Operations Manager I reconciled supplier records against point of sale, inventory and CRM data. For a non profit I mapped 150+ stakeholders, and I tested consumer data with SPSS regression and traced a bus timetable gap to its root cause. Every project starts the same way: ask the right question, find the cause in the data, then recommend the fix.
