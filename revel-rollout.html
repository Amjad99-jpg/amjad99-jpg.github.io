2 hours of payroll admin every week, spent moving hours between manual records and spreadsheets. In 3 months, on £99 a month, I moved the team to a system that sends hours straight to payroll.

01

The problem: 2 hours of admin every week

Staff hours lived in manual records and an Excel sheet, then had to be reported separately for payroll, taking around 2 hours of admin every week.

02

Map it, test it, train it

Phase 1

Process mapping

Mapped how hours were recorded, logged in Excel and reported for payroll.

Why: To see exactly where the 2 hours of admin every week were going.
Phase 2

Set up and test

Set up Revel and tested it before the team switched over.

Why: To catch problems before payroll depended on the new system.
Phase 3

Training

Trained the team on clocking in and out, then moved fully onto Revel.

Why: So the whole team of 7 to 8 could switch over together.
3months, start to finish

03

What changed

  1. 01Manual hours records ended. Staff clock in and out on Revel.
  2. 02Hours feed straight into payroll, with no separate sheet to fill in.
  3. 03Around 2 hours of payroll admin saved every week.
