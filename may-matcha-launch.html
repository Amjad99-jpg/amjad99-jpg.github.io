01

3 questions before spending £9,000

A new matcha brand in Muscat, with a budget of around £9,000, had to answer 3 questions before spending it.

  1. 01Who will buy? Which customers want matcha, and how to reach them.
  2. 02What will they pay? A price that sells and still protects the margin.
  3. 03How do we stand out? Against matcha brands already selling in Muscat.

02

Taste first, launch second

Step 1

Validation

Ran 20+ tastings, including with university students, and collected feedback after each one.

Why: To prove people would buy before spending the £9,000 budget.
Step 2

Drinks

Launched matcha drinks across Muscat, with Instagram marketing tailored to each of the 3 customer groups.

Why: To start selling quickly and adjust pricing and marketing from real customer data.
Step 3

Own brand powder

Launched own brand matcha powder, sourced from Japan, as the second product line.

Why: To add a second product line with a strong profit on every bag.
