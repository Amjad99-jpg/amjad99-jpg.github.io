I launched May Matcha in Muscat as a 3 phase programme over 9 months, from validation to drinks to own brand powder sourced from Japan. Customer data set the range and the pricing.

Launched matcha drinks as the first product line across Muscat.

Launched own brand matcha powder sourced from Japan as the second product line.
