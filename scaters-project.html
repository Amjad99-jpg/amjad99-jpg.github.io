Project 05 · Process mapping

45 products. One old warehouse.
A plan to speed it up.

In a business simulation run on an enterprise (ERP) system, I was Production Manager for Scaters, a Liverpool skateboard manufacturer up against much bigger brands. I mapped the business process end to end, set the production targets and recommended how to track stock.

01 · The problem

A small player losing its speed advantage

Scaters used to win by shipping faster than the big brands. That edge was disappearing.

6%UK market share

Scaters competed with larger manufacturers such as Flip at 18% and Karma at 14%.

2 weeksCompetitor delivery

The big brands had cut their delivery times to two weeks or less, closing the gap.

1990Ageing warehouse

One warehouse with old technology and weak stock control. Finding the right part had once taken an hour.

02 · Process map

From supplier to customer feedback

The full business process in eight steps, with the new tracking step where stock control was weakest.

  1. 1Buy raw materials from suppliers
  2. 2Scan in with RFID and barcodesNew control point
  3. 3Move raw materials to the warehouse
  4. 4Transfer to the factory for production
  5. 5Assemble components into the final product
  6. 6Store finished boards in the warehouse
  7. 7Ship to market
  8. 8Collect customer feedback
03 · Execution

How I did it, in three moves

01

Mapped the process

Traced every step from supplier to customer feedback in the ERP system, and marked where stock control broke down.

02

Set the targets

80 units a day, 10 days for production and 1 day each for dispatch and invoicing, agreed with the other four team members.

03

Recommended the fix

RFID and barcode tracking for raw materials, new equipment with a conveyor layout, routine maintenance and staff training.

04 · Targets

Order cycle inside two weeks

The targets I set keep production and dispatch within the two weeks the large competitors now deliver in.

05 · Recommendations

What I recommended

01

Track every material

Bring RFID and barcode systems in house to sort and store raw materials, so parts are found in seconds, not hours.

02

Modernise the floor

Buy and install new equipment with a conveyor layout, on a routine maintenance cycle.

03

Train for the change

Hire for the new technology and train existing staff on the new layout and conveyor system.

06 · Next steps

What I'd do next

Measure

Report units a day and cycle times from the ERP every week against the targets.

Pilot

Start RFID on the highest volume raw materials before rolling it out.

Cost it

Build a business case comparing the cost of RFID with the time it saves.
